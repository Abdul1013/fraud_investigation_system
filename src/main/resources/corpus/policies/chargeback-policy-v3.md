
# Chargeback Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CB-003
**Version:** 3
**Effective From:** 2024-01-01
**Classification:** C0

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all card-not-present transactions
processed through the platform.

## 2. Chargeback Eligibility

A chargeback may be filed when:

- The cardholder did not authorize the transaction.
- The goods or services were not received.
- The transaction was processed twice.
- The amount charged differs from the agreed amount.

## 3. Evidence Requirements

For each chargeback, the following evidence must be collected:

| Evidence Type          | Required    | Retention |
| ---------------------- | ----------- | --------- |
| Transaction record     | Yes         | 7 years   |
| Delivery confirmation  | Yes         | 7 years   |
| Customer communication | Yes         | 7 years   |
| Device fingerprint     | Conditional | 7 years   |

## 4. Resolution Timeline

Chargebacks must be resolved within 45 days of receipt.
If evidence is incomplete at day 30, escalate to the
chargeback supervisor.

## 5. Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Fraud Investigation team when:

- The transaction value exceeds ₦5,000,000.
- The customer has more than 3 chargebacks in 90 days.
- The merchant is on the high-risk list.
