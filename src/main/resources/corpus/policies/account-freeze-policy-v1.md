# Account Freeze Policy (synthetic learning fixture)
**Version:** 1
**Classification:** C0
**Effective From:** 2024-01-01

## Evidence required
An account restriction recommendation must identify the suspicious activity and its cited evidence.

## Approval
A human analyst must approve a simulated account freeze. An AI recommendation alone does not authorize it.

## Review
Record the reason, approval identity, and policy version, and provide a review path.
